Six questions every student has asked.

We asked students, wardens and staff what frustrates them most. Here's what they said — and exactly how Campus Pulse answers it.

The question

“I raised a complaint weeks ago. Who is even handling it?”

Our answer

Every complaint is automatically routed by department and location to a named coordinator who owns it end to end. You see their name, their progress and a live status — no more black hole.

The question

“They marked it ‘resolved’ — but nothing was fixed.”

Our answer

Fixes need proof photos, and each photo is fingerprinted to catch re-used images and tampered timestamps. You then get 48 hours to confirm or dispute. A dispute sends the on-site Warden to inspect.

The question

“What if the problem is urgent — will anyone act in time?”

Our answer

Every ticket carries a deadline by priority — 12h critical, 24h high, 48h medium, 72h low. A scheduler watches the clock every minute and auto-escalates to managers and admins the moment a deadline is missed.

The question

“I'm scared to report ragging or harassment. Won't they know it's me?”

Our answer

Anonymous reports are stored separately from your identity. You get a case code to chat with admins two-way, without logging in. Unsealing identity needs an admin's password re-entry, a written justification, and is permanently audit-logged.

The question

“In a real emergency, who do I even call — and do they know where I am?”

Our answer

One tap on SOS — Medical, Fire or Security — instantly broadcasts your location, block and room to dispatchers via real-time alerts. If no one acknowledges in 10 minutes, it escalates to senior leadership automatically.

The question

“I lost my ID card / laptop. Where do I even start looking?”

Our answer

Built-in Lost & Found: post what you found or lost, claim items, and get notified — all inside the same app, alongside announcements and direct chat with staff.

Anti-fraud verification

What if someone fakes the repair?

Traditional portals trust a click. We trust evidence.

  • Duplicate photo detection. Every image is hashed with SHA-256; a photo used on any other complaint is instantly flagged REUSED.
  • Timestamp tamper check. A “after repair” photo taken before the job started is flagged OLDER_THAN_JOB.
  • Skip-streak penalty. Repeatedly closing without proof locks the ticket and forces an in-person Warden inspection.

Privacy by design

Speak up without fear.

Anonymity here isn't a promise — it's how the data is built.

  • Structural separation. The report and the reporter's identity live in different sealed tables.
  • Anonymous two-way chat using only a case code.
  • Tamper-proof audit trail. Any unsealing is permanently recorded and can't be edited or deleted.
